The stage-4 space restriction killed every term carrying a $t_2$ amplitude whose two virtual indices both sit on the operator $\hat E$ (the free, external indices of the equation).  In the CF-F12 construction those amplitudes are not independent: they are the fixed F12 geminal amplitudes of the SP ansatz,
\begin{align}
\tilde t^{ab}_{ij} = \tfrac{3}{8}\langle ab|\hat Q_{12} f_{12}|ij\rangle + \tfrac{1}{8}\langle ab|\hat Q_{12} f_{12}|ji\rangle,
\end{align}
with $a,b$ over the complete virtual space and $\hat Q_{12}$ the strong-orthogonality projector (the geminal vanishes with both indices OBS-vir, which is what made the stage-4 rule exact).  The killed terms are the contribution of these fixed geminal amplitudes to the transcorrelated Hamiltonian -- the \emph{F12 special term}.  This stage brings them back: the killed three-body terms are folded back to two-/one-body pieces with the grouped cumulant template of stage 6 (a density matrix with a virtual index is zero; the pure-density scalar part always vanishes), and the result is substituted into the cumulant-expanded blocks of stage 6.
