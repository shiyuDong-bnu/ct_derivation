In the CF-F12 construction of Yanai the virtual space splits into the OBS virtual space and the CABS$+\infty$ remainder.  We restrict the virtual space to OBS-vir: every virtual index is an OBS-vir index, and the amplitude $t^{ab}_{ij}$ vanishes when both of its indices are OBS-vir.  In the many-body expansions of \texttt{many\_body\_equations.tex} this means:
\begin{itemize}
\item a virtual index contracted with another tensor is OK;
\item a term is zero iff both virtual indices of \emph{one and the same} $t^{ab}_{ij}$ amplitude (or $t^{*}$) sit on the operator $\hat{E}$ -- the term then carries the vanishing amplitude. Two free virtual indices stemming from \emph{different} amplitudes do not kill the term.
\end{itemize}
Each block is shown first as the original expansion with the vanishing terms \underline{underlined}, then as the restricted expansion gathering the nonzero terms.  Blocks still containing operators of rank higher than two are marked; their cumulant expansion is done later.
