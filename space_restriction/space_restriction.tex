In the CF-F12 construction of Yanai the virtual space splits into the OBS virtual space and the CABS$+\infty$ remainder.  We restrict the virtual space to OBS-vir: every external virtual index is an OBS-vir index, and the amplitude $t^{ab}_{ij}$ vanishes when both of its indices are OBS-vir.  In the many-body expansions of \texttt{many\_body\_equations.tex} (taken from the \texttt{expanded\_blocks()} API of \texttt{expand\_commutators.py}) this means:
\begin{itemize}
\item a virtual index contracted with another tensor is OK -- the contraction sum still runs over the full virtual space;
\item a term is zero iff both virtual indices of \emph{one and the same} $t^{ab}_{ij}$ amplitude sit on the operator $\hat{E}$ -- the free indices of the operator are the external (OBS-vir) indices of the equation, so the term carries the vanishing amplitude.  Two free virtual indices stemming from \emph{different} amplitudes do not kill the term.
\end{itemize}
Each block is shown first as the original expansion with the vanishing terms \underline{underlined}, then as the restricted expansion gathering the nonzero terms.  Blocks still containing operators of rank higher than two are marked; their cumulant expansion is done later.

\section{The full expression in the OBS-vir restriction}
The commutator stage assembles the blocks into the transcorrelated Hamiltonian through $O(\lambda^2)$ as the blocks plus their Hermitian conjugates (the anti-Hermitian generator $\hat A = \hat T - \hat T^\dagger$), the Fock double-commutator blocks carrying the factor $1/2$ (see the Assembly section of \texttt{many\_body\_equations.tex}).  Assembling the \emph{restricted} blocks the same way and merging over the exact identities (conjugate pairs with equal canonical form fold into one term, self-conjugate terms keeping coefficient 1 through the $1/2$) gives the transcorrelated Hamiltonian in the OBS-vir restriction:
\begin{align}
\bar H - \hat H &= f^{i_{1}}_{i_{2}} t^{a_{1}}_{i_{1}} t^{a_{2}}_{i_{2}} \hat{E}^{a_{1}}_{a_{2}} \\
&\quad - f^{a_{2}}_{i_{2}} t^{a_{1}}_{i_{2}} t^{a_{2}}_{i_{1}} \hat{E}^{a_{1}}_{i_{1}} \\
&\quad - \tfrac{1}{2}\, f^{a_{2}}_{p_{1}} t^{a_{1}}_{i_{1}} t^{a_{2}}_{i_{1}} \hat{E}^{a_{1}}_{p_{1}} - h^{i_{1}}_{p_{1}} t^{a_{1}}_{i_{1}} \hat{E}^{a_{1}}_{p_{1}} \\
&\quad - f^{a_{2}}_{i_{2}} t^{a_{1}}_{i_{2}} t^{a_{2}}_{i_{1}} \hat{E}^{i_{1}}_{a_{1}} + f^{a_{1}}_{a_{2}} t^{a_{1}}_{i_{1}} t^{a_{2}}_{i_{2}} \hat{E}^{i_{1}}_{i_{2}} \\
