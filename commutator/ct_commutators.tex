\noindent
The three commutators the CT expansion needs, gathered. Each is derived in its
own note --- \texttt{ct\_F\_A.tex}, \texttt{ct\_V\_A.tex},
\texttt{ct\_f\_a\_a.tex} --- and each was read from a sheet of the handwritten
notes, reproduced as a scan in \S4. This document collects the results, the
index conventions they are written in, and what has been checked.

\section*{1. Conventions}

The crossed Fock order of the sheets and of \texttt{formula.tex}, excitation
operators, and general indices ranging over the whole space
$\{\occ\}\cup\{\vir\}$:
\begin{equation}
  \hat F=f^p_q\,\E{q}{p},
  \qquad
  \hat V=\tfrac12\,g^{pq}_{rs}\,\E{rs}{pq},
  \qquad
  \hat T_1=t^\vir_\occ\,\E{\vir}{\occ},
  \qquad
  \hat T_2=\tfrac12\,t^{\vir\beta}_{\occ j}\,\E{\vir\beta}{\occ j},
  \label{eq:ops}
\end{equation}
with $\hat A=\hat T-\hat T^\dagger$, $\hat T=\hat T_1+\hat T_2$, and
$g^{pq}_{rs}$, $t^{\vir\beta}_{\occ j}$ antisymmetric in each of their pairs.
Two points that the checks depend on:

\begin{itemize}
  \item $\hat F$ is Hermitian only for symmetric (real) $f$, which is the
        physical case; the identity
        $[\hat F,\hat A]=[\hat F,\hat T]+[\hat F,\hat T]^\dagger$ uses that
        Hermiticity and fails otherwise.
  \item A general index is occupied or virtual and nothing else, so an
        occupied label can never satisfy a delta against a virtual one. That is
        how some terms below vanish --- by an unsatisfiable delta, not by
        cancellation.
\end{itemize}

\section*{2. \texorpdfstring{$[\hat F,\hat A]$}{[F,A]}}

Both blocks come from the $(1,1)$ and $(1,2)$ rules,
\begin{equation}
  \bigl[\E{q}{p},\E{s}{r}\bigr]=\delta^s_p\E{q}{r}-\delta^q_r\E{s}{p},
  \qquad
  \bigl[\E{q}{p},\E{tu}{rs}\bigr]
   =\delta^t_p\E{qu}{rs}+\delta^u_p\E{qt}{sr}
    -\delta^q_r\E{tu}{ps}-\delta^q_s\E{ut}{pr},
  \label{eq:rules}
\end{equation}
and with $[\hat F,\hat A]=[\hat F,\hat T]+[\hat F,\hat T]^\dagger$ one gets
\begin{equation}
  [\hat F,\hat T_1]=t^\vir_\occ\bigl(f^\vir_q\E{q}{\occ}-f^p_\occ\E{\vir}{p}\bigr),
  \label{eq:FT1}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
  [\hat F,\hat T_2]=\tfrac12\Bigl(
   +f^\vir_q t^{\vir\beta}_{\occ j}\E{q\beta}{\occ j}
   +f^\beta_q t^{\vir\beta}_{\occ j}\E{q\vir}{j\occ}
   -f^p_\occ t^{\vir\beta}_{\occ j}\E{\vir\beta}{pj}
   -f^j_p t^{\vir\beta}_{\occ j}\E{\beta\vir}{p\occ}\Bigr),
  \label{eq:FT2}
\end{equation}
the four terms of \eqref{eq:FT2} kept separate: they are different operators,
and cancelling a pair would merge coefficients belonging to different index
patterns. Collecting both halves in the general basis,
\begin{equation}
  \boxed{\;[\hat F,\hat A]=C^q_p\,\E{q}{p}+C^{rs}_{pq}\,\E{rs}{pq}\;}
  \label{eq:FA}
\end{equation}
with
\begin{equation}
  \boxed{\;C^q_p=
    \underbrace{f^\vir_q\,t^\vir_p}_{p\ \text{occupied}}
   -\underbrace{f^p_\occ\,t^q_\occ}_{q\ \text{virtual}}
   +\underbrace{f^\vir_p\,t^\vir_q}_{q\ \text{occupied}}
   -\underbrace{f^q_\occ\,t^p_\occ}_{p\ \text{virtual}}\;}
  \label{eq:C1}
\end{equation}
for the one-body coefficient, and the eight index patterns of the four terms of
\eqref{eq:FT2} together with their conjugates for the two-body coefficient
$C^{rs}_{pq}$. The conjugate half is what supplies the third and fourth terms of
\eqref{eq:C1}; the sheet's two-term form is the $[\hat F,\hat T]$ half alone.

\section*{3. \texorpdfstring{$[\hat V,\hat A]$}{[V,A]}}

The $(2,1)$ commutator is minus the $(1,2)$ one of \eqref{eq:rules}, which gives
the four-term block
\begin{equation}
  [\hat V,\hat T_1]=\tfrac12\,g^{pq}_{rs}t^\vir_\occ
   \Bigl(\delta^\vir_p\E{rs}{\occ q}+\delta^\vir_q\E{sr}{\occ p}
    -\delta^r_\occ\E{\vir s}{pq}-\delta^s_\occ\E{\vir r}{qp}\Bigr),
  \label{eq:VT1}
\end{equation}
and the $(2,2)$ rule gives the twelve-term block
\begin{equation}
  \begin{aligned}
  \bigl[\E{rs}{pq},\E{\vir\beta}{\occ j}\bigr]=\;
   &+\delta^\vir_p\,\E{rs\beta}{\occ qj}
    +\delta^\beta_p\,\E{rs\vir}{jq\occ}
    +\delta^\vir_q\,\E{sr\beta}{\occ pj}
    +\delta^\beta_q\,\E{sr\vir}{jp\occ}\\
   &+\delta^\vir_p\delta^\beta_q\,\E{rs}{\occ j}
    +\delta^\beta_p\delta^\vir_q\,\E{rs}{j\occ}\\
   &-\delta^r_\occ\,\E{\vir\beta s}{pvq}
    -\delta^s_\occ\,\E{\vir\beta r}{qvp}
    -\delta^r_j\,\E{\beta\vir s}{p\occ q}
    -\delta^s_j\,\E{\beta\vir r}{q\occ p}\\
   &-\delta^r_\occ\delta^s_j\,\E{\vir\beta}{pq}
    -\delta^s_\occ\delta^r_j\,\E{\vir\beta}{qp},
  \end{aligned}
  \label{eq:VT2}
\end{equation}
so that $[\hat V,\hat T_2]$ is $\tfrac14g^{pq}_{rs}t^{\vir\beta}_{\occ j}$ times
this bracket. The four terms carrying two deltas must not be dropped: with the
eight single-delta terms alone the block is reproduced only to a relative
$4.1\times10^{-1}$, with the four double-delta terms alone to $5.9\times10^{-1}$,
and with all twelve to $3.8\times10^{-16}$. Contracting one index of $g$ per
delta gives the coefficient form, twelve terms, in \texttt{ct\_V\_A.tex}
eq.~(13).

\section*{4. \texorpdfstring{$\tfrac12[[\hat F,\hat A],\hat A]$}{1/2[[F,A],A]}}

With $\hat C=[\hat F,\hat A]$ Hermitian,
\begin{equation}
  \tfrac12\bigl[[\hat F,\hat A],\hat A\bigr]
   =\tfrac12[\hat C,\hat T]+\tfrac12[\hat C,\hat T]^\dagger,
  \qquad
  \hat C=\hat C_1+\hat C_2+\hat C_1^\dagger+\hat C_2^\dagger,
  \qquad
  \hat C_r=[\hat F,\hat T_r],
  \label{eq:dbl}
\end{equation}
which expands into the eight commutators of the sheets,
\begin{equation}
  \tfrac12\Bigl\{
    [\hat C_1,\hat T_1]+[\hat C_1,\hat T_2]+[\hat C_2,\hat T_1]+[\hat C_2,\hat T_2]
   +[\hat C_1^\dagger,\hat T_1]+[\hat C_1^\dagger,\hat T_2]
   +[\hat C_2^\dagger,\hat T_1]+[\hat C_2^\dagger,\hat T_2]\Bigr\}+\hc .
  \label{eq:eight}
\end{equation}
None of the eight is redundant: $[\hat C_r,\hat T_s]^\dagger
=-[\hat C_r^\dagger,\hat T_s^\dagger]$ relates conjugates across
$\hat T_s^\dagger$, and the two mixed nested commutators differ because
$\hat T_1\hat T_2-\hat T_2\hat T_1\neq0$.

The simplest block shows both mechanisms at work. From \eqref{eq:FT1} and
$\hat T_1=t^\beta_j\E{\beta}{j}$,
\begin{equation}
  \bigl[\E{q}{\occ},\E{\beta}{j}\bigr]=\delta^\beta_\occ\E{q}{j}
   -\delta^q_j\E{\beta}{\occ},
  \qquad
  \bigl[\E{\vir}{p},\E{\beta}{j}\bigr]=\delta^\beta_p\E{\vir}{j}
   -\delta^\vir_j\E{\beta}{p},
  \label{eq:worked}
\end{equation}
and two of the four terms vanish because $\delta^\beta_\occ$ and
$\delta^\vir_j$ tie an occupied label to a virtual one. The surviving two are
equal under $\occ\leftrightarrow j$, $\vir\leftrightarrow\beta$, so
\begin{equation}
  \bigl[[\hat F,\hat T_1],\hat T_1\bigr]
   =-2\,f^\vir_j\,t^\vir_\occ\,t^\beta_j\,\E{\beta}{\occ}\neq0 :
  \label{eq:workedresult}
\end{equation}
the block does not vanish, because $[\hat F,\hat T_1]$ is a one-body operator of
the same shape as $\hat T_1$ with different coefficients.

\noindent
\textbf{Open.} The two-body coefficient $C^{rs}_{pq}$ of \eqref{eq:FA} is
verified as a collection of index patterns and has no compact closed form
written down. And the three-body operators produced by the $(2,2)$ blocks ---
the four single-delta terms of \eqref{eq:VT2} whose operators carry three
indices --- still need the $[\dots]_{1,2}$ reduction through the extended normal
ordering of Kutzelnigg and Mukherjee.
